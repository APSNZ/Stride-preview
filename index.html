Privacy Policy

How Stride collects, uses, stores and protects information.

Prototype notice: this demonstration stores information on the current device and is not a production clinical record system. Passwords in this prototype are stored locally for demonstration only. A live release must use secure server-side authentication, password hashing, encryption, access controls, audit logging, backup and breach-response processes before patients use it for clinical information.

Who is responsible

Advanced Podiatry Solutions is responsible for information collected through Stride. Privacy questions, access requests, correction requests or complaints can be sent to the clinic Privacy Officer at info@advancedpodiatry.co.nz or 09 212 9250.

Information Stride may collect

  • Name, email address and account details.
  • Journey phase, goals, starting pain score, check-ins, symptoms, exercise completion and patient-entered notes.
  • Prescribed resources added by QR code, notification settings, language and accessibility preferences.
  • Technical and security information required to operate and protect a production account, such as login activity, device information and error logs.

Why information is collected

Information is collected to provide the app, support the patient’s agreed care, display prescribed resources, track self-reported progress, provide reminders, maintain account security, troubleshoot faults and improve Stride. Only information reasonably necessary for those purposes should be collected.

Use and disclosure

Information should only be used for the purpose it was collected for, or another purpose permitted by the Privacy Act 2020 and Health Information Privacy Code 2020. It is not sold to advertisers. It may be shared with authorised APS team members, contracted technology providers who need it to operate the service, or another party where the patient authorises this or the law permits or requires it.

Storage and security

A production version must use reasonable safeguards against loss, unauthorised access, use, modification or disclosure. These safeguards should include encrypted transmission, encrypted storage where appropriate, securely hashed passwords, role-based access, multi-factor authentication for administrators, audit logs, secure backups, vulnerability management and staff confidentiality controls.

Access, correction and deletion

Patients may ask APS for access to personal or health information held about them and request correction where information is inaccurate. Requests will be handled under the Privacy Act and Health Information Privacy Code. Some clinical information may need to be retained under applicable health-record retention requirements even after an account is closed.

Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as it is lawfully required or needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Clinical health records may be subject to longer legal retention requirements. Non-clinical app preferences and technical logs should use documented retention periods.

Overseas services

If a production version uses providers that store or process information outside New Zealand, APS must assess those arrangements, explain them to patients where required and ensure appropriate safeguards for overseas disclosure.

Privacy breaches

APS will assess suspected privacy breaches, contain and investigate them, and notify affected people and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner when legally required.

Complaints

Patients can raise a concern with APS first. They may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner if they are not satisfied with the response.

Last updated

4 August 2026. This policy must be reviewed again before any production release or material change to the way Stride handles information.

Terms of Use

Important information about using Stride.

  • Stride supports an agreed care plan and does not replace an assessment or medical advice.
  • Only complete exercises or techniques prescribed and demonstrated by your podiatrist.
  • Contact the clinic if symptoms worsen, a new concern develops, or an exercise causes unexpected pain.
  • Do not use the app for emergencies. Seek urgent medical care when appropriate.
  • Keep access to your device secure if you record personal health information.
By using Stride, you acknowledge that app content is general support information and must be used alongside guidance from your healthcare provider.

What is FMT?

Foot Mobilisation Therapy

FMT is a hands-on approach used to help improve movement through the foot and ankle joints. At APS, it is combined with exercises, education and regular progress reviews to support your wider plan.
